About MediHub

MediHub is an Online Pharmacy and Prescription Management Platform designed to simplify the process of purchasing medicines while ensuring safety, authenticity, and regulatory compliance.

The platform allows patients to upload prescriptions, pharmacists to verify and approve orders, and delivery personnel to provide efficient medicine delivery services.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable, and convenient access to medicines through digital healthcare solutions.

Key Modules

  • Admin Management
  • Customer/Patient Management
  • Pharmacist Verification
  • Prescription Management
  • Stock Management
  • Order & Payment Processing
  • Delivery Tracking
  • Notification System
